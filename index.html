❤️ 25 Romantic Shayari ❤️

Mohabbat ke khoobsurat alfaaz 💕

1. Teri Muskaan ❤️

Teri muskaan meri pehchaan ban gayi,
Teri khushi meri jaan ban gayi,
Kuch is tarah basa hai tu dil mein,
Teri yaad meri zubaan ban gayi.

2. Tera Saath 💖

Tera saath mile toh har raah haseen hai,
Tere bina ye duniya thodi gamgeen hai,
Tu paas ho toh dil ko sukoon mile,
Teri mohabbat hi meri sabse pyari zameen hai.

3. Dil Ki Baat 💕

Dil ki har dhadkan tera naam leti hai,
Teri yaad har subah ek paigaam deti hai,
Kaise bataun kitni mohabbat hai tujhse,
Meri khamoshi bhi tera naam leti hai.

4. Chand Sa Chehra 🌙

Chand se bhi pyara tera chehra hai,
Teri aankhon mein khwaab sunahra hai,
Jab bhi dekhu tujhe pyaar badhta hai,
Tu hi mere dil ka sabse pyara pehra hai.

5. Pehli Mohabbat 💘

Pehli nazar mein dil ko sukoon aaya,
Teri baaton mein pyaar nazar aaya,
Jab se mile ho tum zindagi mein,
Har lamha mujhe khaas nazar aaya.

6. Teri Aankhein 👀

Teri aankhon mein ek jahaan dikhta hai,
Tere chehre par mera armaan dikhta hai,
Jab bhi dekhta hoon tujhe pyaar se,
Mujhe apna poora aasman dikhta hai.

7. Pyara Ehsaas 💞

Tera naam sunte hi chehra khil jaata hai,
Dil mera khushi se bhar jaata hai,
Ye kaisa rishta hai tere saath,
Har pal tera ehsaas yaad aata hai.

8. Khwaab 🌹

Mere khwaabon mein tera aana hai,
Teri yaadon mein kho jaana hai,
Zindagi ke har khoobsurat pal mein,
Bas tera saath nibhaana hai.

9. Mohabbat Ka Rang ❤️

Mohabbat ka rang dil par chha gaya,
Tera khayal har pal aa gaya,
Socha tha sambhal lenge dil ko,
Par tera naam hi labon par aa gaya.

10. Teri Yaadein 💗

Teri yaadein dil ke paas rehti hain,
Teri baatein har ehsaas ke saath rehti hain,
Door hokar bhi door nahi lagte tum,
Tumhari yaadein har pal saath rehti hain.

11. Dil Ka Sukoon 💓

Tujhse milkar sukoon milta hai,
Tere saath har pal khilta hai,
Kya khoob hai ye rishta apna,
Jismein dil har roz sambhalta hai.

12. Tera Naam 🌷

Mere dil ki kitaab mein tera naam hai,
Meri har dua mein tera paigaam hai,
Lafzon mein kaise bayan karun tujhe,
Tu hi meri khushi, tu hi mera armaan hai.

13. Saath Hamesha 💑

Har mod par tera saath chahiye,
Khushiyon mein tera haath chahiye,
Zindagi chahe jaisi bhi ho,
Bas tera pyaar din-raat chahiye.

14. Pyar Bhari Shaam 🌆

Shaam suhaani aur tera khayal ho,
Dil mein mohabbat, labon par sawaal ho,
Kaash har shaam aisi ban jaaye,
Jab tu mere paas bemisaal ho.

15. Teri Hansi 😊

Teri hansi se din mera ban jaata hai,
Tera chehra dil ko bha jaata hai,
Tu khush rahe hamesha zindagi mein,
Mera dil bas yahi dua karta hai.

16. Dil Ki Duniya 💝

Dil ki duniya mein tera basera hai,
Teri mohabbat ka rang gehra hai,
Tu saath ho toh har pal khaas lage,
Tere bina har manzar adhoora hai.

17. Ek Mulaqat 🌹

Tujhse milne ki tamanna rehti hai,
Dil mein teri hi tasveer rehti hai,
Ek mulaqat se dil nahi bharta,
Tujhse milne ki khwahish rehti hai.

18. Tera Ehsaas 💞

Hawaon mein tera paigaam lagta hai,
Har lamha tere naam lagta hai,
Jab se mohabbat hui hai tumse,
Har din ek naya inaam lagta hai.

19. Pyar Ki Baarish 🌧️

Baarish ki boondon mein tera khayal aaye,
Har boond mujhe teri yaad dilaye,
Kaash tu hota mere paas,
Aur ye mausam mohabbat ban jaaye.

20. Tera Intezaar ⏳

Tere aane ki aas rehti hai,
Dil mein ek pyaas rehti hai,
Tu mile toh waqt tham sa jaaye,
Bas isi pal ki talaash rehti hai.

21. Meri Khushi 💖

Teri khushi mein meri khushi hai,
Teri hansi mein meri zindagi hai,
Tera har sapna poora ho,
Mere dil ki bas yahi bandagi hai.

22. Pyara Rishta 🫶

Na vaadon ka bojh, na lafzon ka shor,
Bas dil se dil ka rishta ho,
Har din izzat aur pyaar se bhara,
Apna sa ek khoobsurat farishta ho.

23. Tere Liye 🌺

Tere liye har khushi maangta hoon,
Teri hansi ki dua maangta hoon,
Tu rahe hamesha khush apni duniya mein,
Main bas tera achha saath maangta hoon.

24. Haseen Pal ✨

Tere saath har pal suhaana hai,
Tere saath har sapna sajaana hai,
Zindagi ke khoobsurat safar mein,
Tere saath har lamha bitaana hai.

25. Sirf Tum ❤️

Meri muskaan ka raaz ho tum,
Mere dil ki ek awaaz ho tum,
Lafzon mein kya likhun tumhare liye,
Mere liye bahut hi khaas ho tum.
